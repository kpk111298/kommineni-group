Clinics and health plan, a Kommineni Group business

Meel Care

Meel Care will run clinics and an insurance plan, with patients created by Synthea, the open source patient simulator.

How it runs

An electronic health record for visits, labs and prescriptions, plus a claims system.

  • Patients
  • Visits
  • Lab results
  • Prescriptions
  • Claims

What makes its data hard

  • Hospital records arrive as deeply nested FHIR files.
  • Patient details must be stripped before analysts see the data.
  • Claims get adjusted weeks after the visit.
  • The same patient registers at three clinics under three spellings.

Status

Opening later. Meel Care is planned. Its systems and first problems are on the engineering roadmap.
