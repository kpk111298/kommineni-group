Loans and payments, a Kommineni Group business

Meel Pay

Meel Pay will approve car loans and process card payments for the rest of the group.

How it runs

A loan system for applications and approvals, and a payment processor for charges and refunds.

  • Loan applications
  • Loans
  • Payments
  • Refunds
  • Chargebacks

What makes its data hard

  • Interest rates from the Federal Reserve change approvals overnight.
  • Card numbers and identity details must never reach the warehouse unmasked.
  • Suspicious payments need flagging in seconds, not the next morning.

Status

Opening later. Meel Pay is planned. Its systems and first problems are on the engineering roadmap.
