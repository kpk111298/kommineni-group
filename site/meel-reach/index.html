Marketing, a Kommineni Group business

Meel Reach

Meel Reach will run marketing for the whole group, from ad campaigns to email and website tracking.

How it runs

A marketing platform with an API for campaigns and spend, plus click events from the websites.

  • Campaigns
  • Ad spend
  • Email sends
  • Web clicks

What makes its data hard

  • The ad platform returns 100 rows at a time and blocks too many requests.
  • Millions of clicks a day, some counted twice.
  • Finance and marketing count customers differently.

Status

Opening later. Meel Reach is planned. Its systems and first problems are on the engineering roadmap.
